About this data

Figures come from HESA’s Table 1: HE student enrolments by HE provider, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Every year was checked against HESA’s published UK total before being used here.

HESA rounds every figure to the nearest 5 to protect individual students, so breakdowns may not add up exactly to the total. For universities with very few students, percentages can swing sharply because of this rounding.

HESA’s data has grown to cover more providers over time. A gap in the early years usually means a provider wasn’t included in HESA’s data yet, not that it had no students. For the same reason, this page compares universities with themselves and with each other rather than showing a UK-wide trend.

“Similar-sized providers” are those in the same size band in the most recent year. The typical figure is the median, the middle value, so a few unusual providers can’t pull it up or down.
